Rico Marlow washed up in Port Palmera with a duffel bag, a fake name and one contact: Mara Voss, the fixer who runs the marina. Run her errands, lift the right cars, dodge Captain Ortiz and her Harbor Patrol, and fight the Palm Row crew for the docks — five jobs stand between Rico and owning a piece of the harbor.
